## Accomplishments
- Reusable presentational `GalleryManager` owning drag reorder, optimistic order, pending-upload skeletons, per-tile removal, and the shared upload UX/toasts — with all IO injected as `onUpload`/`onDelete`/`onReorder` callbacks and the `cap` threaded to `acceptImageFiles` + the at-capacity message (Pitfall 1, never the 25 default).
- `PropertyGalleryManager` reduced to a thin wrapper: it keeps `usePropertyDetail()` + `trpc.properties.gallery.*` + `presignPropertyImage` + `MAX_GALLERY_IMAGES` and renders `<GalleryManager cap={25} … />`; the property gallery behaves unchanged (build + typecheck green).
- `AmenityPicker` — a near-twin of `TagPicker` — supports three modes (staged `value`/`onChange`, live `propertyId`, live `unitId`), scope-filters catalog suggestions to the target + `both`, wires the correct property/unit attach/addFreeform/remove/promote mutations with D-38 invalidation, and renders denormalized chips uniformly (D-23).

## Decisions Made
- **Callback shape over raw mutations for GalleryManager.** The plan offered either; callbacks keep the extracted component free of any tRPC/property coupling (verified by the absent-coupling grep), and let it own the gallery-generic toasts while consumers own IO + invalidation.
- **Unit single-attach via `unit.bulkAdd`.** The plan listed `amenities.unit.set` for unit context, but the picker attaches one amenity at a time and `set` replaces all catalog-backed rows. `bulkAdd({ unitIds:[unitId], organizationAmenityIds:[id] })` is idempotent and additive, preserving existing assignments (D-32). `set` remains the right call for the future multi-select bulk sheet (Plans 07/08).
- **Omitted the "Manage amenities…" overlay entry** that `TagPicker` has, since no amenity catalog overlay is registered yet. Everything else mirrors `TagPicker` structurally.

## Deviations from Plan

None affecting scope or correctness. Two discretionary shape choices are logged under Decisions Made (unit single-attach uses `bulkAdd` not `set`; the manage-overlay link is omitted). Both are within the plan's stated latitude ("Claude's discretion") and neither changes the server contract.
